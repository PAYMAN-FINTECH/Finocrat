FASTag Recharge Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: {{ currentDate }}

❌

1. No Cancellations

All recharges are final. We do not support cancellation or change of any FASTag recharge after it is successfully processed. Please ensure you enter the correct vehicle number and FASTag provider before initiating payment.

⚠️ Double-check your vehicle number and provider before making payment!
💸

2. Refunds for Failed Transactions

If your money was deducted but the recharge failed or was not credited to your FASTag wallet, a refund will be initiated automatically or upon request within 5-7 working days after confirmation.

✅ Automatic refund within 5-7 working days
📧 Manual refund on request with proper documentation
⚠️

3. Incorrect Information

We are not responsible for incorrect vehicle number or provider details submitted by users. Please verify all data before completing payment.

Before you pay, verify:

  • ✓ Vehicle number is correct (format: XX00XX0000)
  • ✓ FASTag provider is selected correctly
  • ✓ Mobile number is registered with FASTag
  • ✓ Recharge amount is accurate
📝

4. Requesting a Refund

To raise a refund request for failed transactions, email us at operations@thefinocrat.com with the following details:

🆔
Transaction ID Your unique transaction reference number
🚗
Vehicle Number Your registered vehicle number
📅
Recharge Date Date when the transaction was made
💰
Amount The recharge amount
🏦
FASTag Provider Your FASTag issuing bank/partner

📧 Sample Email Format:

Subject: Refund Request for Failed FASTag Recharge

Transaction ID: TXN123456789
Vehicle Number: MH12AB1234
Recharge Date: {{ currentDate }}
Amount: ₹500
FASTag Provider: HDFC Bank

⚖️

5. Dispute Resolution

Any refund-related disputes will be subject to applicable laws and handled under the jurisdiction of our operational city or as per our partner bank's guidelines.

For any disputes, please contact our support team within 7 days of the transaction.
