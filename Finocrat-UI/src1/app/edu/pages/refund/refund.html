Refund Policy

Last updated: January 29, 2026

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We stand behind the quality of our courses. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date, no questions asked.

Eligibility for Refunds

To be eligible for a refund, the following conditions must be met:

  • The refund request must be submitted within 30 days of purchase
  • You must have completed less than 30% of the course content
  • The course was not purchased during a promotional period with special terms
  • You have not previously received a refund for the same course
  • You have not violated our Terms of Service

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our support team through the Contact Page
  • Provide your order number and registered email address
  • Briefly explain the reason for your refund request (optional but helpful)

Our support team will review your request within 2–3 business days.

Refund Processing Time

Once your refund request is approved:

  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days
  • The refund will be credited to your original payment method
  • Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take an additional 3–5 business days for the amount to reflect in your account

Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for refunds:

  • Courses purchased more than 30 days ago
  • Courses with more than 30% content completed
  • Subscription renewals (you may cancel to avoid future charges)
  • Course bundles where individual courses have been accessed
  • Certificates of completion that have already been issued

Course Access After Refund

Once a refund is processed, you will immediately lose access to the course, including videos, learning materials, certificates, progress data, and notes. All associated data will be permanently removed.

Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be granted at our discretion for course bundles where only some courses were accessed, or in cases of verified technical issues that significantly impacted your learning experience.

Subscription Cancellations

For monthly or annual subscription plans:

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
  • No refunds are provided for partial subscription periods
  • You will retain access until the end of your paid period

Course Completion Certificate & Refunds

Course Completion Certificates are issued only after successful completion of all required tests and assessments.

  • Certificates are not issued if a refund is requested or processed
  • Refunds are not applicable once a certificate has been issued
  • Requesting a refund invalidates eligibility for certification

Technical Issues

If you experience technical issues that prevent access to course content, please contact our support team before requesting a refund. If the issue cannot be resolved, you may be eligible for a full refund regardless of the 30-day window.

Gift Purchases

Courses purchased as gifts may be refunded within 30 days if the gift recipient has not redeemed the course. Once redeemed, the standard refund policy applies.

Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Refund Policy or need assistance, please contact our support team through the Contact Page.
